About Wear Relaxed

Wear Relaxed is built around a simple idea: style doesn’t have to look forced.

We document the people, outfits, and everyday moments shaping modern relaxed streetwear — starting in Los Angeles and expanding wherever the culture takes us.

Our focus is on real style in the real world: oversized silhouettes, easy layers, vintage influence, sneakers, workwear, luxury, and the individual details that make an outfit feel personal.

What started as a street photography project is becoming something bigger — a platform for discovering the people, brands, and ideas defining a more relaxed way of dressing.

We’re here to document the culture first.
Then help shape where it goes next.
